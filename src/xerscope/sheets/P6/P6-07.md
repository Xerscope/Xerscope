# P6-07 · Relationship Types

Share of Finish-to-Start relationships.

## Population (denominator)
Start: every relationship of the project (its `TASKPRED` rows whose two ends are `TASK` rows of the file).
1. into a task: the successor is a task, not a WBS summary (model)
2. not both ends complete: predecessor and successor not both `TK_Complete` (101248)

## Counted (numerator)
`TASKPRED.pred_type` = `PR_FS`. The list shows the others (`PR_SS`, `PR_FF`, `PR_SF`),
not the counted ones; the note gives the mix (e.g. `FS 41, SS 6, FF 3`).

Value = FS / population × 100. Target `> 90%`: strictly greater, so exactly 90 % fails.

## Source
- [P6 Professional help (101248)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18296_01/client_help/en_US/101248.htm):
  the target is the share of relationships that must be Finish to Start; relationships
  between completed tasks are not included.
- [P6 EPPM help, dialog defaults (91574)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18294_01/p6help/en/91574.htm):
  greater than 90 %.

## Example
| Pred. | Succ. | `pred_type` | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| A100 | A110 | `PR_FS` | counted |
| A110 | A120 | `PR_SS` | checked, listed |
| A120 | A130 | `PR_FS` | counted |

2 FS / 3 checked = 66.7 %: fails `> 90%`.
