# P6-12 · Late activities

Open activities forecast to finish after their baseline finish.

## Population (denominator)
Activities not complete (`TASK.status_code` is not `TK_Complete`), not milestones
(`TT_Mile`, `TT_FinMile`), not level of effort (`TT_LOE`). Needs a baseline (sidebar).

## Counted (numerator)
Finish later than the baseline finish by more than 0 working hours, measured on the
activity's current calendar (`TASK.clndr_id`). Finish = P6's Finish column (actual, else
`TASK.reend_date`, else `TASK.early_end_date`, else `TASK.target_end_date`). An activity absent from the baseline is
compared with its own planned finish `TASK.target_end_date`.

Column `finish_variance_wd` = that variance in working days of the activity's calendar.

Value = counted / population × 100. Target `< 5%`.

## Source
- [P6 Professional help (101248)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18296_01/client_help/en_US/101248.htm):
  later than the corresponding baseline activity; no baseline counterpart fails when its
  remaining early finish is later than its planned finish; milestones, WBS summary and level
  of effort not included.
- [P6 EPPM help, Late Activities section (46278)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18294_01/p6help/en/46278.htm):
  activities with an actual finish are removed; variance in hours. A P6 Professional report
  (R1, `docs/standards.md`) leaves completed activities out too.

## Baseline to choose
"Planned dates in each file (target_*)" reproduces P6 when the project is its own baseline
(`docs/standards.md`, P6 section).

## Example
| Activity | Calendar | BL finish | Finish | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A100 | 8 h, Mon–Fri | Fri 17:00 | Mon 08:00 | checked, not counted (0 working h) |
| A110 | 8 h, Mon–Fri | Fri 17:00 | Mon 17:00 | counted, 1.0 wd |

1 counted / 2 checked = 50.0 %.

## Change it
Target only: `target` of `P6-12` in `standards/p6.toml`. A tolerance: the rule
`late_vs_baseline` with `tolerance_hours` in your own standard.
