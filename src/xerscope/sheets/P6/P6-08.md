# P6-08 · Lags

Relationships with a positive lag.

## Population (denominator)
Start: every relationship of the project (its `TASKPRED` rows whose two ends are `TASK` rows of the file).
1. into an activity: the successor is an activity, not a WBS summary (model)
2. not both ends complete: predecessor and successor not both `TK_Complete` (101248)

## Counted (numerator)
`TASKPRED.lag_hr_cnt` > 0. A negative lag (lead) is P6-09's.

Value = counted / population × 100. Target `< 5%`: exactly 5 % fails.

## Source
- [P6 Professional help (101248)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18296_01/client_help/en_US/101248.htm):
  relationships with positive lag; relationships between completed activities are not included.

## Example
| Pred. | Succ. | `lag_hr_cnt` | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| A100 | A110 | 16 | counted |
| A110 | A120 | 0 | checked, not counted |
| A120 | A130 | -8 | checked, not counted (lead: P6-09) |

1 counted / 3 checked = 33.3 %.
