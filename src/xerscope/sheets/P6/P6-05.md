# P6-05 · Long lags

Relationships with a lag above x.

## Population (denominator)
Start: every relationship of the project (its `TASKPRED` rows whose two ends are `TASK` rows of the file).
1. into an activity: the successor is an activity, not a WBS summary (model)
2. not both ends complete: predecessor and successor not both `TK_Complete` (101248)

## Counted (numerator)
`TASKPRED.lag_hr_cnt` > `limit_hours` (blank lag = 0). x = `limit_hours = 352` in `p6.toml`.

Value = counted / population × 100. Target `< 5%`.

## Source
- [P6 EPPM help, Long Lags section (46288)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18294_01/p6help/en/46288.htm):
  links between two activities that both have an actual finish are removed; the limit is the
  number of hours set in the dialog.
- [P6 Professional help (101248)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18296_01/client_help/en_US/101248.htm):
  a value entered in days or weeks is converted to hours with the Hours per Time Period settings.

## Example
| Pred. | Succ. | Statuses | `lag_hr_cnt` | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A100 | A110 | Complete → Complete | 400 | out of population |
| A110 | A120 | Complete → Not started | 400 | counted |
| A120 | A130 | Not started → Not started | 352 | checked, not counted |

1 counted / 2 checked = 50.0 %.

## Change it
x: `params = { limit_hours = 352 }` of `P6-05` in `standards/p6.toml`, in hours. Target: `target`.
