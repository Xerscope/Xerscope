# P6-15 · Out of sequence

Started activities whose predecessor is still open.

## Population (denominator)
All activities (WBS summaries excluded), as the P6 Professional report divides.

## Counted (numerator)
Activities started (`TASK.status_code` is not `TK_NotStart`) with at least one predecessor
that is still open at the data date:
- FS (`PR_FS`): the predecessor is not complete;
- SS (`PR_SS`): the predecessor has not started.

The list has one row per (activity, predecessor), columns `predecessor` and `type`, as P6
lists them; the count is of activities.

Value = counted / population × 100. Target `< 2%`, set in `p6.toml`: Oracle documents no default.

## Source
- [P6 Professional help, Relationships and Assignments tab (113174)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G48902_01/client_help/en_US/113174.htm):
  activities started or in progress before their predecessor is completed. The page gives no
  population.
- The population, the pairs and the FS / SS reading come from a P6 Professional report
  (R1, `docs/standards.md`). Open: FF and SF links (`UNRESOLVED` in the rule's docstring).

## Example
| Activity | Status | Predecessor (type, status) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| A110 | In progress | A100 (FS, in progress) | counted |
| A120 | In progress | A100 (SS, in progress) | checked, not counted |
| A130 | Not started | A120 (FS, in progress) | checked, not counted |

1 counted / 4 activities (with A100) = 25.0 %.

Target only: `target` of `P6-15` in `standards/p6.toml`.
