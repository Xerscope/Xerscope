# P6-09 · Negative Lags

Relationships with a negative lag (lead).

## Population (denominator)
Start: every relationship of the project (its `TASKPRED` rows whose two ends are `TASK` rows of the file).
1. into a task: the successor is a task, not a WBS summary (model)
2. not both ends complete: predecessor and successor not both `TK_Complete` (101248)

## Counted (numerator)
`TASKPRED.lag_hr_cnt` < 0.

Value = counted / population × 100. Target `< 1%`.

## Source
- [P6 Professional help (101248)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18296_01/client_help/en_US/101248.htm):
  lag duration less than 0; relationships between completed tasks are not included.

## Example
| Pred. | Succ. | Statuses | `lag_hr_cnt` | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A100 | A110 | Complete → Not started | -8 | counted |
| A110 | A120 | Complete → Complete | -8 | out of population |
| A120 | A130 | Not started → Not started | 0 | checked, not counted |

1 counted / 2 checked = 50.0 %.
