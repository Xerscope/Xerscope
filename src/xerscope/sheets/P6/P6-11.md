# P6-11 · Resource / cost

Activities with nothing assigned: no resource, role or expense.

## Population (denominator)
All activities except milestones (`TT_Mile`, `TT_FinMile`) and level of effort (`TT_LOE`).
Completed activities stay in.

## Counted (numerator)
No `TASKRSRC` row (resource or role, any `TASKRSRC.rsrc_type`) and no `PROJCOST` row
(expense) for the activity. Units or costs on the assignment are not looked at.

Value = counted / population × 100. Target `< 1%`.

## Source
- [P6 Professional help (101248)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G18296_01/client_help/en_US/101248.htm)
  and the Relationships and Assignments tab
  ([113174](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G48902_01/client_help/en_US/113174.htm)): an activity passes
  with at least one resource, role or expense, whatever its cost; milestones, WBS summary and
  level of effort are not included. Completed activities are not excluded.

## Example
| Activity | Status | `TASKRSRC` | `PROJCOST` | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A100 | Complete | – | – | counted |
| A110 | Not started | role only | – | checked, not counted |
| A120 | Not started | – | 1 expense | checked, not counted |
| M900 | – | – | – | out of population (milestone) |

1 counted / 3 checked = 33.3 %.

## Change it
Target only: `target` of `P6-11` in `standards/p6.toml`. Without expenses or with another
population: the rule `no_resource` in your own standard (`exclude`, `expenses_count`).
